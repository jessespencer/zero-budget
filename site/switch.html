From YNAB

Import the Register CSV from a YNAB export. Multi-account files are matched to your Zero accounts by name.

From your bank

Download a CSV from your bank or card. Ten formats are detected automatically. Anything else maps column by column with a live preview.

Starting fresh

No history to bring? A setup checklist walks you through accounts, a starter set of categories and your first assignment.

What comes over

  • Every transaction, with its date, payee, amount and memo
  • Categories, when the name matches one you've set up in Zero
  • Accounts, matched by name on multi-account exports

What doesn't

  • Assigned amounts and targets — you budget fresh from this month
  • Rows whose category Zero can't match land as uncategorized, flagged for a quick pass
